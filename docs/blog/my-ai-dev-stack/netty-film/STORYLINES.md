# Storylines: Netty, end to end

The one thing: one developer and a team of agents took Netty from an idea to a launch, with the developer directing.
Facts used: idea.md (Oct 27), 38 commits on Oct 27 from 12:25 to 22:58, three agents in herdr worktrees, the landing reviewed in ego-browser, the launch with launch-skills, published by Oct 30.

## B (recommended): One sentence, all the way through

- Device and form: one object across apps, as an object-to-object chain.
- The opening line of idea.md ("tells you at a glance whether you are really on the internet") travels through the whole film. It becomes the prompt the agents get in herdr, then commits, then the landing headline in ego-browser ("A dot in your Mac's menu bar"), then the launch post, and finally the green dot itself.
- First 2 s: idea.md in the Obsidian Inbox, the cursor selecting that line; it lifts off the page.
- Beats: hook (idea.md) → tension (the line split across three agents, each working) → reveal (Oct 27, 12:25 → 22:58, 38 commits stream by) → proof (the line lands as the landing hero, the agent checks it in ego-browser) → payoff (the launch post goes out, the line shrinks into the green menu bar dot) → claim ("I direct. They build.") → end card (Netty dot + "Oct 27 → Oct 30").
- Look: Ghostty's dark terminal ground, SF Pro and SF Mono, the Netty status colours (green, yellow, red) as the only accents.
- Sound: soft electronic bed, key clicks on each commit, one chime when the dot turns green.

## C (the bold one): The dot

- Device and form: the result first, as match cuts on one shape.
- Every tool in the stack shows a status dot: Netty in the menu bar, herdr in its sidebar, the agent cursor in ego-browser. The film runs backwards through them. Each dot is a door into the step before it, until the last dot is the cursor blinking in idea.md.
- First 2 s: the green Netty dot in a real menu bar, the camera diving into it.
- Beats: hook (green dot) → the dot becomes herdr's agent dots (three agents working) → the agent's yellow dot, "needs you" → the ego-browser cursor dot over the landing → the commit stream, rewinding → idea.md, Oct 27, 12:25 → claim → end card (back out to the green dot).
- Look: pure black between dots, each world at full brightness, the dot always centred at the cut.
- Sound: a low pulse that speeds up with each dive, silence on idea.md, the chime on the way out.

## A: One day, one team

- Device and form: a moment in someone's day, as split screen to one.
- A clock reads Oct 27, 12:25. The day plays as split panes: idea.md, three agents in herdr, the landing in ego-browser, the commit log. The panes collapse into the one that matters at each hour, until 22:58, and then three days skip to the launch.
- First 2 s: the clock and idea.md side by side, the first agent starting.
- Beats: hook (12:25, idea.md) → tension (four panes busy at once) → reveal (the team named: Ghostty, herdr, Gentle AI) → proof (each pane takes the screen in turn) → payoff (22:58, last commit, the landing live) → claim → end card.
- Look: a 2×2 grid on the Ghostty ground, pane borders in the Netty green.
- Sound: a ticking clock under a light bed, one cut per tick.
